# Definition Of Done

Purpose: Define when an AzubiForge AP1 chapter is truly complete.

This document is mandatory for every chapter.

---

## Core Rule

A chapter is done only when it helps the student:

- understand the topic,
- explain it in simple German,
- recognize it in AP1 tasks,
- solve exercises,
- avoid common mistakes,
- and decide whether they are ready.

Reading text is not enough. A chapter must create exam readiness.

---

## 1. Technical Quality

A chapter is technically done when:

- All explanations are correct.
- Simplifications are accurate and not misleading.
- Technical terms are used correctly.
- Comparisons are valid.
- Examples are realistic.
- No important AP1 concept is missing.
- No advanced detail distracts from AP1.

Reject if:

- The chapter contains half-truths.
- The explanation is too vague.
- Examples do not match real IT work.

---

## 2. Educational Quality

A chapter is educationally done when:

- It teaches from zero.
- It explains why the concept exists.
- It explains how the concept works.
- It shows where companies use it.
- It connects to previous chapters.
- It prepares for later chapters.
- It contains real-world and practical examples.
- It includes common mistakes.

Reject if:

- It only defines terms.
- It assumes too much prior knowledge.
- It does not teach step by step.

---

## 3. Language Quality

A chapter is linguistically done when:

- German is the main language.
- German level is B1/B2.
- Technical German terms are included.
- Difficult terms are explained.
- Portuguese support helps understanding.
- Portuguese does not replace the German learning path.

Reject if:

- German is too academic.
- Portuguese becomes the main content.
- Vocabulary is missing examples.

---

## 4. AP1 Relevance

A chapter is AP1-ready when:

- It explains how the topic appears in AP1.
- It includes AP1 task verbs.
- It includes exam traps.
- It explains frequently confused concepts.
- It contains AP1-style exercises.
- It helps students answer scenario-based questions.

Reject if:

- It is technically interesting but not AP1-relevant.
- It lacks exam-oriented guidance.

---

## 5. Examples

A chapter is example-complete when it includes:

- Real-world company examples.
- Practical step-by-step examples.
- At least one AP1-style scenario.
- Examples that use German vocabulary.

Reject if:

- Examples are abstract.
- Examples are decorative.
- Examples do not help solve tasks.

---

## 6. Diagrams

A chapter is diagram-complete when:

- It includes a diagram if the topic has structure, flow, layers or relationships.
- The diagram supports the explanation.
- The diagram is simple enough for beginners.
- The text explains how to read the diagram.

Reject if:

- A visual topic has no diagram.
- Diagram is too complex.
- Diagram is unrelated to AP1.

---

## 7. Exercises

A chapter is exercise-complete when it includes:

- 5 Easy exercises.
- 5 Intermediate exercises.
- 5 AP1-style exercises.
- Explained answers for all exercises.
- Exercises that test understanding, not only memorization.

Reject if:

- Answers are only one word.
- Explanations are missing.
- AP1-style tasks are not scenario-based.

---

## 8. Vocabulary

A chapter is vocabulary-complete when:

- It includes at least 8 AP1-relevant German terms.
- Each term has Portuguese support.
- Each term has a German explanation.
- Each term has an example sentence.

Reject if:

- Vocabulary is just a translation list.
- Example sentences are missing.

---

## 9. Revision Material

A chapter is revision-complete when it includes:

- One-page summary.
- Mind map.
- Common mistakes.
- Revision checklist.
- Related chapters.

Reject if:

- Student cannot revise quickly.
- Summary only repeats the introduction.

---

## 10. Review And Publication Approval

A chapter is publishable only after:

- Technical review.
- Educational review.
- German review.
- AP1 relevance review.
- Final checklist.

Final approval statement:

"This chapter is ready to be used as primary AP1 study material."

If this statement cannot be honestly made, the chapter is not done.

---

## 11. Student Readiness

A student is ready after a chapter when they can answer YES:

- Can I explain this topic in simple German?
- Can I recognize the topic in an AP1 task?
- Can I solve easy questions?
- Can I solve AP1-style questions?
- Can I compare this topic with similar concepts?
- Can I explain common mistakes?

If the student answers NO, the chapter must guide revision.
